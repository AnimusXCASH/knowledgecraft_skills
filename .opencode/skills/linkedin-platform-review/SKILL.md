# LinkedIn Platform Review
Keep substance intact. Check audience relevance, first-screen clarity, mobile readability, restrained/relevant hashtags, genuinely relevant mentions, natural CTA, and content-format fit.

Do not assume external links are always penalized; treat link placement as a testable choice. Avoid engagement bait. Platform behavior changes, so current ranking claims that materially affect a decision should be verified against current official guidance rather than encoded as permanent folklore.
