# Research Source Grounder
Extract title, author/organization, date, purpose/question, sample/context, methods where relevant, findings, numbers/statistics, examples, limitations, and practical caveats.

Every reusable statement must be typed as `fact`, `inference`, `opinion`, or `proposal`, with evidence location, confidence, and reuse permission.

Preserve numbers, units, dates, populations, conditions, technical terms, and causal strength. Do not generalize beyond the source or strengthen association into causation. Keep conflicting claims separate. Do not draft promotional copy here.
