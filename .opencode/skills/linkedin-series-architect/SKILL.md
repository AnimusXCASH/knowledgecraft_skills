# LinkedIn Series Architect
Give each post a distinct reader job: setup, teach, challenge, evidence, real story, application, synthesis, or conversation.

Use a small number of coherent content pillars. Avoid consecutive posts with the same job or the same claim cluster. Keep continuity subtle unless numbered parts genuinely help. Put prerequisite ideas before dependent ones.

Flag posts that repeat the same supporting claims, opening mechanism, example, conclusion, CTA, or reader job. Follow user cadence constraints; otherwise propose a sustainable range, not an algorithmic “best” frequency. Return the series plan before mass drafting.
