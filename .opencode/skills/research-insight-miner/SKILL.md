# Research Insight Miner
For each idea define audience, audience problem/decision/misconception, core insight, supporting claim IDs, practical or conceptual consequence, angle, novelty reason, evidence strength, input needed from a practitioner/author, and risk/limitation notes.

Useful angle families when supported: practical implication, misconception correction, counterintuitive result, decision framework, trade-off, mechanism, limitation, application question, evidence gap, or before/after way of thinking.

Merge/discard ideas that share the same claim cluster, audience problem, takeaway, example, and reader action. Never invent a personal anecdote.
