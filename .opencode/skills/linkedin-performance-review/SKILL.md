# LinkedIn Performance Review
Keep raw counts and calculate available rates such as reaction, comment, share, save, click-through, profile-view, follow, or lead rate.

Compare cautiously for post age, network size, topic, format, time/day, paid vs organic, and external events. Classify learnings as `strong observation`, `tentative pattern`, `test next`, or `insufficient data`.

Say “Evidence-led posts had a higher median save rate in this sample,” not “LinkedIn rewards evidence-led posts.” Prefer one-major-variable-at-a-time future tests. Do not erase the author’s voice to chase short-term metrics.
