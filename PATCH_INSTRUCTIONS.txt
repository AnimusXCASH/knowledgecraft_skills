USAGE
-----
Full reset:
.\scripts\reset-workspace.ps1 -Full

Research only:
.\scripts\reset-workspace.ps1 -Research

Content only:
.\scripts\reset-workspace.ps1 -Content

Analytics only:
.\scripts\reset-workspace.ps1 -Analytics

Scratch only:
.\scripts\reset-workspace.ps1 -Scratch

Add -Force to skip the RESET confirmation prompt.

IMPORTANT
---------
A full reset deletes generated .knowledgecraft state, including the local research registry.
It does NOT delete:
- .opencode/skills/
- AGENTS.md
- papers/

After a full reset, papers still in ./papers/ will be seen as unprocessed/new when scanned again.
